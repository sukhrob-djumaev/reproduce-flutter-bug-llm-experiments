# Banner cannot be dismissed after leaving and returning to the list

In the accessibility-assessment sample app, the home list is filtered by default and does not show every item. Open the filter menu (top-right), select "batch1" and deselect "batch2", then close the menu; this makes the "MaterialBanner" entry visible in the list.

Steps:
1. Open the "MaterialBanner" entry.
2. Tap "Show a MaterialBanner".
3. Without dismissing the banner, press the back arrow in the app bar to return to the list.
4. Tap the banner's "DISMISS" button.

Expected: The banner disappears.

Actual: The banner stays on screen.
